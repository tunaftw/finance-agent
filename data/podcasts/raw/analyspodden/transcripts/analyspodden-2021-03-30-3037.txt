[22:27] Yeah. So as we touched upon earlier in the introduction, you're also an entrepreneur and investor. So in your opinion, what is the most important element then to succeed with a start-up company today?
[22:46] Yeah. I mean, there's a number of different factors. I think probably if I were to identify a couple of the key ones, I would say it is about rapid iteration and learning. You know, I think in the old days when you created a company, there wasn't as much infrastructure around how to create a company. So you would raise a large amount of money. So it's a true self-confidence that allows you to start the company and a true drive and a passion, but the willingness to have that self-confidence to listen to other people. So that's probably one core aspect of it. I think the second thing is, is focusing on the problem, not the solution, which is core to that learning process. So the more you can focus on what the problem is, as opposed to the solution, the more you're able to dynamically adapt your solution to the problem, as opposed to thinking that you have the one solution to that problem. Because one of the things, if you look at the network of text businesses, like a Facebook or a Google, when you do search, the fact that one billion people search for the same thing as you before you search for it, allows Google to serve you and provide you with better results. So that concept of learning from the collective experience of others and providing the best solution provides value, and network of text businesses are the best example of that. So that same approach applies starting a company, which is if you're trying to sell something to a thousand customers, like if you're an enterprise sales product or a billion customers because you're a consumer product, the more you can get their input and feedback on how they view the problem and what they would like to see solved, what frictions they would like to be reduced, the better off your product is going to be. So that's probably a second core aspect of it. And I think the third core aspect is focusing on doing that in a way that's profitable. A lot of times you see the press and other people saying like, oh, these technology companies, they're not profitable, it's all fake. But in reality, there's two types of profit. There's contribution margin and then there's bottom line profit.
