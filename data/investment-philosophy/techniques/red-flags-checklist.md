# Red Flags Checklist

## Quick Reference

A comprehensive checklist for identifying warning signs in company filings. Use this during every filing analysis to catch potential problems before they become disasters.

---

## How to Use This Checklist

1. **Review each category** while analyzing a filing
2. **Mark any flags** that appear
3. **Weight the findings** - one flag is a concern, multiple flags are serious
4. **Document evidence** - cite specific line items or disclosures
5. **Compare to peers** - is this company an outlier?

---

## Revenue Quality Flags

### Accounts Receivable

- [ ] **DSO increasing** - Days Sales Outstanding growing faster than revenue
- [ ] **AR growing faster than revenue** - Collection issues or channel stuffing
- [ ] **Large bad debt write-offs** - Previous recognition was premature
- [ ] **Receivables from related parties** - Potential bogus revenue
- [ ] **Factoring/selling receivables** - Hiding collection problems (check footnotes)

**Calculation:**
```
DSO = (Accounts Receivable / Revenue) × 365
```

### Revenue Recognition

- [ ] **Complex revenue recognition policy** - More complex = more manipulation opportunity
- [ ] **Policy changes** - Switching methods often indicates problems
- [ ] **Bill-and-hold arrangements** - Revenue before delivery
- [ ] **Long-term contract assumptions** - Aggressive percentage-of-completion
- [ ] **Barter transactions in revenue** - Non-cash revenue is lower quality
- [ ] **Unusual revenue spikes at quarter-end** - Stuffing the channel

### Revenue Sustainability

- [ ] **Heavy reliance on one-time gains** - Non-operating income in operating results
- [ ] **Customer concentration** - Top customer > 20% of revenue
- [ ] **Revenue from related parties** - Self-dealing concerns
- [ ] **Licensing/royalty revenue spikes** - May not recur

## Earnings Quality Flags

### Accruals & Cash Flow

- [ ] **Net Income >> Operating Cash Flow** - Accruals-heavy earnings
- [ ] **Persistent OCF < Net Income** - Multi-year divergence is serious
- [ ] **Negative free cash flow with positive earnings** - Something doesn't add up
- [ ] **Working capital swings** - Unusual changes in receivables, inventory, payables

**Sloan Accruals Ratio:**
```
Accruals = (Net Income - Operating Cash Flow) / Total Assets
High ratio (>10%) = Low quality earnings
```

### Non-GAAP Metrics

- [ ] **Large gap between GAAP and adjusted earnings** - What are they adjusting?
- [ ] **"Adjusted" metrics exclude recurring costs** - Stock comp, restructuring
- [ ] **Changing non-GAAP definitions** - Moving the goalposts
- [ ] **Non-GAAP EPS much higher than GAAP** - Consistent pattern of adjustments

### One-Time Items

- [ ] **Frequent "non-recurring" charges** - If it happens every year, it's recurring
- [ ] **Gains classified as operating, losses as non-operating** - Cherry-picking
- [ ] **Big bath restructuring charges** - Setting up future beats
- [ ] **Asset sales coinciding with earnings misses** - Manufacturing income

## Balance Sheet Flags

### Assets

- [ ] **Goodwill > 50% of assets** - Overpaid acquisitions, impairment risk
- [ ] **Intangibles growing rapidly** - Capitalizing what should be expensed
- [ ] **Inventory growing faster than sales** - Obsolescence risk
- [ ] **Capitalized costs increasing as % of revenue** - Deferring expenses
- [ ] **Unusual "other assets" line items** - Hidden problems

### Liabilities

- [ ] **Off-balance sheet arrangements** - Check footnotes for SPEs, VIEs
- [ ] **Operating lease obligations** - Future commitments (add to debt)
- [ ] **Pension underfunding** - Hidden liability
- [ ] **Warranty reserves declining as sales grow** - Under-reserving
- [ ] **Deferred revenue swings** - Cookie jar reserves
- [ ] **Litigation contingencies disclosed but not reserved** - May be worse than stated

### Equity

- [ ] **Excessive stock compensation** - Diluting shareholders
- [ ] **Treasury stock shenanigans** - Buybacks funded by debt
- [ ] **Retained earnings declining** - Paying out more than earning
- [ ] **Comprehensive income << Net income** - Hidden losses in OCI

---

## Cash Flow Flags

### Operating Cash Flow

- [ ] **OCF declining while revenue grows** - Working capital consuming cash
- [ ] **Large difference between OCF and Net Income** - Accruals issue
- [ ] **Cash taxes << income tax expense** - Aggressive tax positions
- [ ] **Capitalized interest** - Adds to assets, not expensed

### Investing Cash Flow

- [ ] **Acquisitions masking organic decline** - Buying growth
- [ ] **Capex cuts to boost free cash flow** - Underinvesting
- [ ] **Asset sales every quarter** - Monetizing the business
- [ ] **Unusual investing "proceeds"** - What are they selling?

### Financing Cash Flow

- [ ] **Debt increasing to fund dividends/buybacks** - Unsustainable returns
- [ ] **Frequent equity issuances** - Dilution, cash needs
- [ ] **Short-term borrowing increasing** - Liquidity concerns
- [ ] **Related party loans** - Self-dealing

---

## Management & Governance Flags

### Insider Activity

- [ ] **Heavy insider selling** - Especially by multiple executives
- [ ] **Executives selling on 10b5-1 plans** - Pre-planned but watch timing
- [ ] **Low insider ownership** - No skin in the game
- [ ] **Generous options/RSUs to insiders** - Self-compensation

### Management Behavior

- [ ] **Frequent accounting policy changes** - Hiding problems
- [ ] **CFO departure** - Finance chief leaving is rarely good
- [ ] **Auditor change** - Especially to smaller firm
- [ ] **Delayed filings** - Material issues being worked through
- [ ] **Guidance cuts followed by management departures** - They knew

### Compensation

- [ ] **Excessive executive compensation** - Misaligned incentives
- [ ] **Targets based on revenue not profit** - Encourages growth at any cost
- [ ] **Adjusted metrics used for bonuses** - Self-serving definitions
- [ ] **Low say-on-pay votes** - Shareholders concerned

### Board Issues

- [ ] **Related party transactions** - Self-dealing
- [ ] **Board lacks independence** - Captured by management
- [ ] **Audit committee red flags** - Financial expertise lacking
- [ ] **Frequent board turnover** - Disagreements with management

---

## Industry-Specific Flags

### Technology/Software

- [ ] **Capitalized software development costs increasing** - Deferring R&D
- [ ] **Deferred revenue declining** - Backlog concerns
- [ ] **Customer churn increasing** - Retention problems
- [ ] **Billings << Revenue** - Booking revenue not yet contracted

### Retail

- [ ] **Same-store sales declining** - Core business weakening
- [ ] **Inventory turns slowing** - Obsolescence risk
- [ ] **Lease obligations large vs earnings** - Real estate burden
- [ ] **Channel inventory builds** - Stuffing retailers

### Financial Services

- [ ] **Loan loss provisions declining** - Under-reserving
- [ ] **Non-performing assets increasing** - Credit quality issues
- [ ] **Off-balance sheet exposures** - Hidden risk
- [ ] **Deposit outflows** - Liquidity concerns

### Manufacturing

- [ ] **Capacity utilization declining** - Fixed cost leverage problem
- [ ] **Customer concentration** - Dependency risk
- [ ] **Warranty claims increasing** - Quality issues
- [ ] **Inventory obsolescence risk** - Technology changes

## Severity Scoring

### High Severity (Immediate Concern)

- Auditor resignation
- Delayed filings
- CFO departure
- Restatements
- SEC investigation
- Massive insider selling

### Medium Severity (Requires Monitoring)

- DSO/inventory trends deteriorating
- Net income >> OCF for 2+ years
- Frequent restructuring charges
- Acquisitions masking organic decline
- Related party transactions

### Low Severity (Context Dependent)

- Single quarter anomalies
- Industry-wide issues
- Explained one-time items
- Minor policy changes
