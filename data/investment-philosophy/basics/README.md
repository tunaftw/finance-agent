## Quick Reference Cards

### The Accounting Equation

```
Assets = Liabilities + Shareholders' Equity
```

Everything in accounting flows from this.

### The Three Statements at a Glance

| Statement | Shows | Key Question |
|-----------|-------|--------------|
| **Income Statement** | Profitability over a period | "Did we make money?" |
| **Balance Sheet** | Financial position at a point in time | "What do we own and owe?" |
| **Cash Flow Statement** | Cash movements over a period | "Where did the cash go?" |

### Most Important Metrics (Quick List)

| Metric | Formula | Good Value |
|--------|---------|------------|
| **Free Cash Flow** | OCF - CapEx | Positive, growing |
| **ROE** | Net Income / Equity | >15% consistently |
| **Debt/Equity** | Total Debt / Equity | <1.0 typically |
| **Current Ratio** | Current Assets / Current Liab | >1.5 |
| **Gross Margin** | Gross Profit / Revenue | Industry-dependent |

### Red Flags to Watch

- Net Income >> Operating Cash Flow (accruals problem)
- Receivables growing faster than revenue
- Inventory building up
- Frequent "one-time" charges
- Related party transactions
- Off-balance sheet arrangements

## Swedish/IFRS Quick Notes

Swedish companies use IFRS (International Financial Reporting Standards), which differs from US GAAP in several ways:

| Area | IFRS | US GAAP |
|------|------|---------|
| Revenue | IFRS 15 | ASC 606 (similar) |
| Leases | All on balance sheet | All on balance sheet (now) |
| R&D | Some capitalization allowed | Mostly expensed |
| Inventory | No LIFO allowed | LIFO allowed |
| Impairment | Can reverse | No reversal |

See [ifrs-vs-gaap.md](reading-filings/ifrs-vs-gaap.md) for full details.
