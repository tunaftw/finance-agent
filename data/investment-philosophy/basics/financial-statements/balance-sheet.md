# Balance Sheet (Balansräkning)

## Quick Answer

The balance sheet shows a company's **financial position at a specific point in time** - what it owns (assets), what it owes (liabilities), and the residual value belonging to shareholders (equity). It always balances: Assets = Liabilities + Equity.

---

## The Fundamental Equation

```
Assets = Liabilities + Shareholders' Equity

What we OWN = What we OWE + What belongs to OWNERS
```

This must always balance. If it doesn't, something is wrong.

---

## Structure

```
ASSETS (Tillgångar)
├── Current Assets (Omsättningstillgångar)
│   ├── Cash & Equivalents (Likvida medel)
│   ├── Accounts Receivable (Kundfordringar)
│   ├── Inventory (Varulager)
│   └── Prepaid Expenses (Förutbetalda kostnader)
└── Non-Current Assets (Anläggningstillgångar)
    ├── Property, Plant & Equipment (Materiella anläggningstillgångar)
    ├── Intangible Assets (Immateriella tillgångar)
    ├── Goodwill
    └── Investments (Finansiella tillgångar)

LIABILITIES (Skulder)
├── Current Liabilities (Kortfristiga skulder)
│   ├── Accounts Payable (Leverantörsskulder)
│   ├── Short-term Debt (Kortfristiga lån)
│   ├── Accrued Expenses (Upplupna kostnader)
│   └── Deferred Revenue (Förutbetalda intäkter)
└── Non-Current Liabilities (Långfristiga skulder)
    ├── Long-term Debt (Långfristiga lån)
    ├── Pension Liabilities (Pensionsskulder)
    └── Deferred Tax Liabilities (Uppskjuten skatteskuld)

SHAREHOLDERS' EQUITY (Eget kapital)
├── Common Stock (Aktiekapital)
├── Additional Paid-in Capital (Överkursfond)
├── Retained Earnings (Balanserade vinstmedel)
└── Other Comprehensive Income (Övrigt totalresultat)
```

---

## Key Line Items Explained

### Current Assets (Omsättningstillgångar)

**What it is**: Assets expected to be converted to cash within 1 year.

**Key items**:
- **Cash**: Actual cash and short-term investments
- **Receivables**: Money owed by customers (collected within 30-90 days typically)
- **Inventory**: Goods held for sale

**Watch for**:
- Receivables growing faster than revenue = collection issues
- Inventory building up = demand problem or obsolescence

### Non-Current Assets (Anläggningstillgångar)

**What it is**: Long-term assets (>1 year useful life).

**Key items**:
- **PP&E**: Buildings, machinery, equipment (depreciated over time)
- **Intangibles**: Patents, trademarks, software (amortized)
- **Goodwill**: Premium paid in acquisitions (tested for impairment)

**Watch for**:
- Goodwill > 50% of assets = acquisition-heavy, impairment risk
- Capitalized costs growing = may be deferring expenses

### Current Liabilities (Kortfristiga skulder)

**What it is**: Obligations due within 1 year.

**Key items**:
- **Payables**: Money owed to suppliers
- **Short-term debt**: Loans due within a year
- **Deferred revenue**: Cash received but not yet earned

**Watch for**:
- Payables growing fast = stretching suppliers (cash flow issues?)
- Large deferred revenue = good backlog

### Long-term Liabilities (Långfristiga skulder)

**What it is**: Obligations due beyond 1 year.

**Key items**:
- **Long-term debt**: Bonds, term loans
- **Lease liabilities**: Now on balance sheet under IFRS 16
- **Pension obligations**: Future payments to retirees

**Watch for**:
- Total debt levels vs. EBITDA
- Pension underfunding

### Shareholders' Equity (Eget kapital)

**What it is**: The residual interest - what's left for shareholders after all liabilities.

**Key items**:
- **Share capital**: Par value of issued shares
- **Retained earnings**: Accumulated profits not paid as dividends

**Formula**:
```
Shareholders' Equity = Total Assets - Total Liabilities
```

## Important Metrics from Balance Sheet

| Metric | Formula | Good Value |
|--------|---------|------------|
| **Current Ratio** | Current Assets / Current Liabilities | >1.5 |
| **Quick Ratio** | (Current Assets - Inventory) / Current Liab | >1.0 |
| **Debt-to-Equity** | Total Debt / Equity | <1.0 typically |
| **Book Value per Share** | Equity / Shares Outstanding | Context-dependent |

## Red Flags

- [ ] **Goodwill >> Tangible assets** - Overpaid for acquisitions?
- [ ] **Receivables growing faster than revenue** - Collection issues
- [ ] **Inventory building up** - Demand problem
- [ ] **Debt/Equity rising** - Increasing leverage
- [ ] **Negative retained earnings** - Accumulated losses
- [ ] **Off-balance sheet items in footnotes** - Hidden obligations
- [ ] **Related party receivables** - Potential bogus transactions

| Retained Earnings | Balanserade vinstmedel |

### IFRS 16 Leases

Under IFRS 16, all leases (except short-term and low-value) are now on the balance sheet:
- **Right-of-use asset** in Non-Current Assets
- **Lease liability** in Liabilities

This increases both assets and liabilities. Compare carefully to US companies pre-2019.

### Revaluation

IFRS allows revaluation of PP&E and intangibles upward (US GAAP doesn't). Watch for this in "revaluation reserve" within equity.
