# Income Statement (Resultaträkning)

## Quick Answer

The income statement shows a company's **profitability over a period** (quarter or year). It starts with revenue at the top and ends with net income at the bottom, which is why net income is called the "bottom line."

---

## Structure

```
Revenue (Nettoomsättning)
- Cost of Goods Sold (Kostnad sålda varor)
─────────────────────────────────────────
= Gross Profit (Bruttoresultat)
- Operating Expenses (Rörelsekostnader)
  - Selling, General & Admin (Försäljnings- och administrationskostnader)
  - Research & Development (Forskning och utveckling)
  - Depreciation & Amortization (Av- och nedskrivningar)
─────────────────────────────────────────
= Operating Income / EBIT (Rörelseresultat)
- Interest Expense (Räntekostnader)
+ Interest Income (Ränteintäkter)
─────────────────────────────────────────
= Pre-tax Income (Resultat före skatt)
- Income Tax (Skatt)
─────────────────────────────────────────
= Net Income (Årets resultat)
```

---

## Key Line Items Explained

### Revenue / Sales (Nettoomsättning)

**What it is**: Money earned from selling products or services.

**Where to find it**: First line of income statement.

**Watch for**:
- Organic growth vs. acquisition-driven growth
- Revenue recognition policies (footnotes)
- Customer concentration

### Cost of Goods Sold (COGS)

**What it is**: Direct costs to produce what was sold (materials, labor, manufacturing).

**Formula**:
```
COGS = Beginning Inventory + Purchases - Ending Inventory
```

**Watch for**:
- COGS growing faster than revenue = margin pressure
- Inventory write-downs buried here

### Gross Profit (Bruttoresultat)

**What it is**: Revenue minus direct costs. Shows basic profitability of the product/service.

**Formula**:
```
Gross Profit = Revenue - COGS
Gross Margin = Gross Profit / Revenue
```

**Good value**: Varies by industry. Software >70%, retail 25-40%, manufacturing 20-35%.

### Operating Income / EBIT (Rörelseresultat)

**What it is**: Profit from core operations, before interest and taxes.

**Formula**:
```
Operating Income = Gross Profit - Operating Expenses
Operating Margin = Operating Income / Revenue
```

**What it tells you**: How efficiently the company runs its core business.

### Net Income (Årets resultat)

**What it is**: The "bottom line" - profit after all expenses including interest and taxes.

**Formula**:
```
Net Income = Revenue - All Expenses - Taxes
Net Margin = Net Income / Revenue
```

**Watch for**:
- Compare to Operating Cash Flow (should be similar over time)
- One-time items that inflate or deflate

---

## Important Metrics from Income Statement

| Metric | Formula | Interpretation |
|--------|---------|----------------|
| **Gross Margin** | Gross Profit / Revenue | Pricing power, cost efficiency |
| **Operating Margin** | Operating Income / Revenue | Operational efficiency |
| **Net Margin** | Net Income / Revenue | Overall profitability |
| **EPS** | Net Income / Shares Outstanding | Per-share profitability |

| Line Item | Amount (MSEK) | % of Revenue |
|-----------|---------------|--------------|
| Revenue | 10,000 | 100% |
| COGS | (6,000) | 60% |
| **Gross Profit** | 4,000 | **40%** |
| SG&A | (2,000) | 20% |
| R&D | (500) | 5% |
| D&A | (300) | 3% |
| **Operating Income** | 1,200 | **12%** |
| Interest Expense | (100) | 1% |
| **Pre-tax Income** | 1,100 | 11% |
| Taxes (22%) | (242) | 2.4% |

## Red Flags

- [ ] **Revenue growth without profit growth** - Are they buying growth?
- [ ] **Declining margins** - Losing pricing power or cost control
- [ ] **One-time gains in operating income** - Should be below the line
- [ ] **Net income >> Operating Cash Flow** - Accruals problem
- [ ] **Unusual "other income"** - What's in there?
- [ ] **Tax rate suddenly lower** - Sustainable?

## Swedish/IFRS Notes

### Terminology Mapping

| English | Swedish | Notes |
|---------|---------|-------|
| Revenue | Nettoomsättning | Net of VAT/discounts |
| COGS | Kostnad sålda varor | |
| Gross Profit | Bruttoresultat | |
| Operating Income | Rörelseresultat | Same as EBIT |
| Pre-tax Income | Resultat före skatt | |
| Net Income | Årets resultat | "Year's result" |

### Key Differences

1. **Presentation**: Swedish reports often show expenses by function (COGS, SG&A) or by nature (materials, personnel, depreciation)

2. **"Övriga rörelseintäkter"**: Other operating income - watch for one-time items here

3. **"Finansiella poster"**: Financial items (interest, FX gains/losses) are shown separately

## Where to Find It

### In 10-K (US)
- Item 8: Financial Statements
- Called "Consolidated Statements of Income" or "Operations"

### In Swedish Annual Report
- "Koncernens resultaträkning" (Group Income Statement)
- Usually pages 30-50 depending on report
