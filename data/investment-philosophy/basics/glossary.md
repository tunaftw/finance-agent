# Glossary of Financial Terms

A comprehensive A-Z reference of accounting and financial analysis terms. Swedish translations included where applicable.

---

## A

### Accounts Payable (Leverantörsskulder)
Money owed to suppliers for goods/services received but not yet paid.

### Accounts Receivable (Kundfordringar)
Money owed by customers for goods/services delivered but not yet collected.

### Accrual Accounting (Periodisering)
Recording revenue when earned and expenses when incurred, regardless of cash timing.

### Accruals Ratio
Measure of earnings quality: `(Net Income - Operating Cash Flow) / Total Assets`. Higher = lower quality.

### Accumulated Depreciation (Ackumulerade avskrivningar)
Total depreciation taken on an asset since acquisition. Reduces gross PP&E to net book value.

### Amortization (Avskrivningar på immateriella tillgångar)
Spreading the cost of intangible assets over their useful life.

### Asset (Tillgång)
Economic resource owned or controlled by a company that provides future benefit.

### Asset Turnover (Kapitalomsättning)
Revenue / Total Assets. Measures how efficiently assets generate sales.

### Audit (Revision)
Independent examination of financial statements by a certified auditor.

---

## B

### Balance Sheet (Balansräkning)
Financial statement showing assets, liabilities, and equity at a point in time. Assets = Liabilities + Equity.

### Book Value (Bokfört värde)
Assets minus liabilities; shareholders' equity. Also called net asset value.

### BVPS (Book Value Per Share)
Shareholders' Equity / Shares Outstanding.

---

## C

### CapEx (Capital Expenditure / Investeringar)
Money spent to acquire, maintain, or improve fixed assets (PP&E).

### Cash Conversion Cycle (CCC)
Days from paying for inventory to collecting from customers: DIO + DSO - DPO.

### Cash Flow Statement (Kassaflödesanalys)
Financial statement showing cash inflows and outflows from operating, investing, and financing activities.

### COGS (Cost of Goods Sold / Kostnad för sålda varor)
Direct costs of producing goods sold (materials, labor, manufacturing overhead).

### Contingent Liability (Eventualförpliktelse)
Potential obligation that may arise depending on outcome of future event (e.g., lawsuit).

### Current Assets (Omsättningstillgångar)
Assets expected to be converted to cash within one year (inventory, receivables, cash).

### Current Liabilities (Kortfristiga skulder)
Obligations due within one year (accounts payable, short-term debt, accrued expenses).

### Current Ratio
Current Assets / Current Liabilities. Measures short-term liquidity.

---

## D

### D&A (Depreciation & Amortization)
Non-cash expenses for tangible (depreciation) and intangible (amortization) asset wear.

### Debt-to-Equity (Skuldsättningsgrad)
Total Debt / Shareholders' Equity. Measures financial leverage.

### Deferred Revenue (Förutbetalda intäkter)
Cash received for goods/services not yet delivered. Liability until earned.

### Deferred Tax (Uppskjuten skatt)
Tax difference between book and tax accounting. Can be asset or liability.

### Depreciation (Avskrivningar)
Systematic allocation of fixed asset cost over useful life. Non-cash expense.

### DIO (Days Inventory Outstanding)
Average days inventory sits before sale: (Inventory / COGS) × 365.

### Diluted EPS
EPS calculated assuming all convertible securities are converted to shares.

### Dividend (Utdelning)
Distribution of profits to shareholders.

### Dividend Yield (Direktavkastning)
Annual Dividend Per Share / Share Price.

### DPO (Days Payables Outstanding)
Average days to pay suppliers: (Accounts Payable / COGS) × 365.

### DSO (Days Sales Outstanding)
Average days to collect from customers: (Accounts Receivable / Revenue) × 365.

### DuPont Analysis
ROE decomposition: Profit Margin × Asset Turnover × Financial Leverage.

---

## E

### EBIT (Earnings Before Interest and Taxes / Rörelseresultat)
Operating income. Revenue minus operating expenses.

### EBITDA
EBIT + Depreciation + Amortization. Cash proxy for operating performance.

### Enterprise Value (EV / Företagsvärde)
Market Cap + Total Debt - Cash. Total value to acquire a business.

### EPS (Earnings Per Share / Vinst per aktie)
Net Income / Weighted Average Shares Outstanding.

### Equity (Eget kapital)
Owners' residual interest: Assets - Liabilities. Also called shareholders' equity or net worth.

### EV/EBITDA
Enterprise Value / EBITDA. Common valuation multiple.

---

## F

### Fair Value (Verkligt värde)
Estimated market price of an asset or liability.

### FASB
Financial Accounting Standards Board. Sets US GAAP standards.

### FCF (Free Cash Flow / Fritt kassaflöde)
Cash available after maintaining capital assets: Operating Cash Flow - CapEx.

### FIFO (First In, First Out)
Inventory method: oldest inventory sold first.

### Fixed Assets (Anläggningstillgångar)
Long-term assets used in operations (PP&E, intangibles).

### Footnotes (Noter)
Detailed disclosures in financial statements explaining accounting policies and specifics.

### FVOCI (Fair Value through Other Comprehensive Income)
IFRS classification for financial assets measured at fair value with changes in OCI.

### FVTPL (Fair Value through Profit or Loss)
IFRS classification for financial assets measured at fair value with changes in income.

---

## G

### GAAP (Generally Accepted Accounting Principles)
US accounting standards set by FASB.

### Goodwill
Excess of acquisition price over fair value of net identifiable assets. Tested annually for impairment.

### Gross Margin (Bruttomarginal)
(Revenue - COGS) / Revenue. Profitability before operating expenses.

### Gross Profit (Bruttoresultat)
Revenue - COGS.

---

## H

### HTM (Held to Maturity)
US GAAP classification for debt securities held until maturity, measured at amortized cost.

---

## I

### IASB
International Accounting Standards Board. Sets IFRS standards.

### IFRS (International Financial Reporting Standards)
Global accounting standards used in 140+ countries including EU/Sweden.

### Impairment (Nedskrivning)
Reduction in asset value when fair value falls below book value.

### Income Statement (Resultaträkning)
Financial statement showing revenue, expenses, and profit over a period.

### Intangible Assets (Immateriella tillgångar)
Non-physical assets: patents, trademarks, goodwill, software.

### Interest Coverage Ratio (Räntetäckningsgrad)
EBIT / Interest Expense. Ability to service debt.

### Inventory (Varulager)
Goods held for sale or use in production.

### Invested Capital
Equity + Debt - Cash. Capital employed in the business.

---

## K

### K3
Swedish GAAP for private companies (simplified compared to IFRS).

---

## L

### Leverage (Hävstång)
Use of debt to amplify returns. Also refers to debt ratios.

### Liabilities (Skulder)
Obligations owed to others (debt, payables, deferred revenue).

### LIFO (Last In, First Out)
Inventory method: newest inventory sold first. Not permitted under IFRS.

### Liquidity (Likviditet)
Ability to meet short-term obligations with available cash/assets.

### Long-term Debt (Långfristiga skulder)
Debt due beyond one year.

---

## M

### Market Cap (Börsvärde)
Share Price × Shares Outstanding. Total equity market value.

### MD&A (Management Discussion & Analysis)
Management's explanation of financial results (Item 7 in 10-K).

### Minority Interest (Minoritetsintresse)
Third-party ownership in consolidated subsidiaries.

---

## N

### Net Debt (Nettoskuld)
Total Debt - Cash. Debt burden after available cash.

### Net Income (Årets resultat / Nettoresultat)
Bottom-line profit after all expenses and taxes.

### Net Margin (Nettomarginal)
Net Income / Revenue. Overall profitability.

### Non-current Assets (Anläggningstillgångar)
Assets not expected to convert to cash within one year.

### NOPAT (Net Operating Profit After Tax)
EBIT × (1 - Tax Rate). Operating profit after taxes, before financing.

---

## O

### OCF (Operating Cash Flow / Kassaflöde från löpande verksamhet)
Cash generated from core business operations.

### OCI (Other Comprehensive Income)
Gains/losses bypassing income statement (FX translation, pension adjustments, available-for-sale securities).

### Operating Expenses (Rörelsekostnader)
Costs to run the business: SG&A, R&D, marketing.

### Operating Income (Rörelseresultat)
Revenue - COGS - Operating Expenses. Same as EBIT.

### Operating Margin (Rörelsemarginal)
Operating Income / Revenue.

### Owner Earnings
Buffett's measure: Net Income + D&A - Maintenance CapEx - Working Capital changes.

---

## P

### P/B (Price-to-Book)
Share Price / Book Value Per Share.

### P/E (Price-to-Earnings)
Share Price / EPS. Valuation multiple.

### PEG Ratio
P/E / Earnings Growth Rate. P/E adjusted for growth.

### PP&E (Property, Plant & Equipment / Materiella anläggningstillgångar)
Physical long-term assets: land, buildings, machinery, equipment.

### Preferred Stock (Preferensaktier)
Equity with fixed dividends, priority over common stock.

### Provision (Avsättning)
Liability of uncertain timing or amount (warranties, restructuring, legal).

---

## Q

### Quick Ratio (Likviditetsgrad)
(Current Assets - Inventory) / Current Liabilities. Stricter than current ratio.

---

## R

### R&D (Research & Development / Forskning och utveckling)
Spending on innovation. Expensed under US GAAP, may be capitalized under IFRS.

### Retained Earnings (Balanserade vinstmedel)
Accumulated profits not distributed as dividends.

### Revenue (Intäkter / Nettoomsättning)
Income from sales of goods or services.

### ROA (Return on Assets)
Net Income / Total Assets. How efficiently assets generate profit.

### ROE (Return on Equity / Avkastning på eget kapital)
Net Income / Shareholders' Equity. Return to shareholders.

### ROIC (Return on Invested Capital)
NOPAT / Invested Capital. Return on all capital employed.

---

## S

### SEC
Securities and Exchange Commission. US securities regulator.

### SG&A (Selling, General & Administrative)
Operating expenses for sales, marketing, and corporate functions.

### Share Capital (Aktiekapital)
Par value of issued shares.

### Shareholders' Equity (Eget kapital)
Assets - Liabilities. Owners' residual claim.

### Short-term Debt (Kortfristiga skulder)
Debt due within one year.

---

## T

### Tangible Assets (Materiella tillgångar)
Physical assets: PP&E, inventory.

### Tax Rate (Skattesats)
Income Tax Expense / Pre-tax Income.

### Total Assets (Totala tillgångar)
Sum of all assets on balance sheet.

### Trailing Twelve Months (TTM)
Most recent 12-month period, used for annualized metrics.

### Treasury Stock (Återköpta aktier)
Shares repurchased by the company. Reduces equity.

---

## U

### Unearned Revenue
See Deferred Revenue.

---

## V

### Valuation (Värdering)
Estimating the worth of a business or asset.

---

## W

### WACC (Weighted Average Cost of Capital)
Blended cost of debt and equity financing.

### Working Capital (Rörelsekapital)
Current Assets - Current Liabilities. Short-term operational capital.

### Write-down (Nedskrivning)
Reduction in asset book value.

### Write-off (Avskrivning av fordran)
Complete removal of asset from books (e.g., uncollectible receivable).

---

## X

### XBRL
eXtensible Business Reporting Language. Machine-readable format for SEC filings.

---

## Y

### Yield
Return on investment, typically expressed as percentage (dividend yield, FCF yield).

### YoY (Year over Year)
Comparison to same period in prior year.
